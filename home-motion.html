No mould pulled off a shelf, no catalogue page — a single sheet, drawn out by hand.

01 — The sheet

It starts as a flat sheet

No mould pulled off a shelf, no catalogue page. Four millimetres of cast acrylic, still under its film, cut to the footprint your room asked for.

02 — The pull

One pull, one piece

Heated until it gives, then drawn down in a single vacuum pull. The waste flange stays on for now — proof it came out of the sheet whole, with nothing joined.

03 — The reinforcement

The half you never see

The flange comes off and the outside is backed by hand. Coarse, matte, unphotographed — this is the half that decides whether the tub is still true in twenty years.

04 — The surface

Sanded until it stops being a surface

Worked back through every grade until the eye reads one piece of material rather than something manufactured. One overflow hole is the only interruption.

05 — The finish

Filled with light

Set where the light falls across it, brassware run before a pipe is soldered. This one is yours alone — the drawing is retired the day it ships.

Materials
Your shape, in acrylic or solid surface.

Premium acrylic

Warm · light · luminous
  • CharacterWarm, light and luminous — gloss or matte, in any colour.
  • Weight27–45 kg — a two-person install, suits upper floors and standard joists.
  • ShapeThermoformed over a mould — near-limitless curves.
  • Best forUpper-floor bathrooms, complex curves, lighter budgets.

Solid surface

Dense · seamless · stone-like
  • CharacterDense, stone-like and seamless — a soft matte, monolithic feel.
  • Weight90–150 kg — grounded, substantial presence; we advise on floor loading.
  • WarmthHigh thermal mass — holds heat beautifully on a long soak.
  • Best forGround-floor statement pieces, spa-style long soaks.

We craft both in-house, one piece at a time. Not sure which? Choosing your material →
